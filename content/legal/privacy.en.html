2. Hosting and server logs

The website runs on a server operated by netcup GmbH, Karlsruhe, in Germany, which processes data on behalf of the controller (Art. 28 GDPR). To deliver the website to your browser, the server processes the IP address of your device, the date and time of the request and the address requested (URL).

The server does not record requests. Only if an error occurs while a page is being delivered does it store an error entry containing the IP address, the time and the address requested; these entries are deleted after 14 days.

The purposes are delivering the website and finding errors. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in these purposes.

3. No cookies, no tracking

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking services. All files, including the fonts, are served by the website's own server; nothing is loaded from other providers.

4. Storage in your browser

If you choose a look for the website (light, dark, or red and gold) or change the companions (the pixel figures on the home page: which of them appear and how they look), your choice is saved in your browser's local storage, so that it is kept for your next visit. This information stays on your device and is not sent to the server. Saving it is strictly necessary for these functions, which you use on your own initiative (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser's settings; "Start over" in the companions menu deletes the companions' part.

5. Links to other websites

The website links to LinkedIn, GitHub, the Aresium demo at aresium.de and the contact form at www.ares-empire.de. Data is only transmitted to these websites when you follow a link; from then on, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies, for the contact form the privacy policy of www.ares-empire.de.

6. Your rights

You have the right to

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR).

Right to object: where processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object to it at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise these rights, an email to {{owner.email}} is sufficient.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for the controller is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de.

7. Automated decisions

No automated decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR), takes place.
